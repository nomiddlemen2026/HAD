Your report stays private to you. Nothing is published until a moderator checks it, and your name, address and messages are never shown publicly.

Evidence vault

Every screenshot, chat export, voice note or receipt is fingerprinted (SHA-256) and logged. Originals are never altered, even when you make a redacted copy.

Police-ready summary

Export a case summary PDF with your statement, an evidence index with hashes, a timeline and clearly labelled unverified information.

What H.A.D will not do

  • It cannot track a phone or find someone's location from their number. Nobody can do that from a number alone, and we never pretend to.
  • It does not publish accusations automatically. Every public summary is written by a moderator and checked for personal data.
  • It does not replace the police. If you are in danger, call 999. For scams and money already lost, call the National Scam Response Centre at 997.
