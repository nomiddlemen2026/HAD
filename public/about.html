About H.A.D

H.A.D (Hunt Ahlong Down) is a community platform for people in Malaysia targeted by illegal moneylenders (ahlong), loan scams and harassment. It helps victims organise evidence, see whether a number has been reported before, and give the police a clear, honest record.

What it does

  • Lets victims report threats privately, with evidence that is fingerprinted and never altered.
  • Shows reviewed, masked community reports when you search a number.
  • Reads WhatsApp chat exports into a timeline and flags possibly threatening wording for a person to check.
  • Produces a case summary PDF for the police that labels every unverified claim.

What it does not do

  • It cannot locate anyone from a phone number, WhatsApp message or IP address. Any service that claims it can from a number alone is not telling the truth.
  • It does not decide guilt. A report is an allegation. Published summaries describe what was reported, not who is responsible.
  • It is not the police and is not an official record.

How a report becomes public

  1. A victim files a report. It is private.
  2. A moderator checks the report and the evidence.
  3. If it is supported, the moderator writes a short public summary with no names, addresses or contact details.
  4. Search shows that summary with the number masked, the month, and a warning that numbers can be reassigned.
  5. Anyone can ask for a correction or takedown.
