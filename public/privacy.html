Privacy

Draft for the MVP. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and does not claim compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) or its amendments.

What we collect

  • Your email, display name and password hash, to run your account.
  • Reports, case notes, locations and evidence files you choose to upload.
  • Security logs: login attempts, access to evidence, moderator and admin actions, and IP addresses for abuse prevention.

Who can see it

  • You can see everything in your own cases.
  • Moderators can see reports and evidence when reviewing them. Each access to an evidence file is logged.
  • The public sees only moderator-written summaries, masked numbers, categories and months. Never your name, contact details, address, messages or files.

Locations

Home addresses are stored only to about 1 km and are never displayed publicly. Your own location is never treated as a suspect location.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from the Account page. Your cases and evidence are deleted, unless a case is under a legal hold (for example, at police request), in which case it is kept as the law requires, without your account details.

Corrections

If information about you is wrong, use the corrections page.
