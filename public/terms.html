Terms of use

Draft for the MVP; requires legal review before launch.

  • Only report what you honestly believe happened to you or that you witnessed.
  • Do not use H.A.D to harass, threaten, identify or track anyone, including suspected ahlong.
  • Do not upload other people's private information unless it is part of the threat you received.
  • False or malicious reports will be removed and accounts suspended. Making false accusations may also have legal consequences.
  • Community reports are allegations. H.A.D does not confirm that any person committed a crime.
  • H.A.D is not an emergency service. In danger, call 999.
