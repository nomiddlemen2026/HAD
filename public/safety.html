If an ahlong is threatening you

  1. Do not delete anything. Keep messages, voice notes and call logs. In WhatsApp, open the chat, tap the menu, choose More → Export chat, and save the .txt file.
  2. Make a police report at the nearest station. Bring your phone and any screenshots. Ask for the report number.
  3. Upload your evidence to H.A.D. Each file gets a fingerprint (SHA-256) and a timestamp, so you can show it has not been changed since.
  4. Export your case summary PDF and bring it to the police as an organised index of what happened.
  5. Do not pay to make threats stop. Payments usually lead to more demands.
  6. Warn family members and your workplace if the threats mention them, so they do not engage or pay.

Protecting yourself on H.A.D

  • Use an email address the harasser does not know.
  • Your reports are private. Public search only shows moderator-written summaries with the number masked.
  • You do not need to give your home address. If you do, it is stored only to about 1 km and never shown publicly.
