Phone number intelligence

Search reviewed community reports. Malaysian and international numbers are accepted.

What this search can and cannot tell you

  • It shows only reports a moderator has reviewed. Unreviewed allegations are never shown.
  • The country shown is where the number was issued. It is not where the person is; numbers work overseas.
  • Carrier and subscriber identity are not shown. Numbers can be ported between carriers, so a prefix is not reliable, and subscriber records are only available to the police.
  • Your own reports, with the full number, are in My cases.
